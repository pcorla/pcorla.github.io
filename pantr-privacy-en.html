2. Overview

Pantr is an app for managing shopping lists, recipes and your pantry. The app does not collect personal data for advertising or analytics purposes and does not share any data with advertisers. There is no Pantr account and no provider-operated server: your content lives on your device and — if you are signed in to iCloud — in your own iCloud account. The following explains what data is processed when you use the app and how it is handled.

3. Data Processed

3.1 Data on Your Device and in iCloud

All content you enter — recipes, ingredients, shopping lists, pantry items (including quantities, package sizes and expiry dates) and settings — is stored locally on your device. If you are signed in to an Apple ID on your device, the app automatically synchronises this data with your iCloud account — no configuration required (see 3.2). Without an iCloud account, all data remains exclusively on your device.

3.2 iCloud / CloudKit

If you are signed in to an Apple ID on your device, recipes, shopping lists and pantry items are synchronised across your Apple devices — including a paired Apple Watch — via CloudKit (Apple Inc.) and — if you use the sharing feature — shared with people you choose. Data is stored in your personal iCloud container and is subject to Apple's Privacy Policy. Pantr has no access to this data outside of your device.

You can keep several households side by side and share each of them separately. Within a shared household, other participants can see who added an item or a recipe: for this, Pantr stores the first and last name that Apple provides for your iCloud account through the sharing feature. That name is only ever displayed inside the shared household it belongs to.

Synchronisation via iCloud happens automatically when you are signed in to an Apple ID on your device. It can be disabled in iOS system settings under iCloud. Without an iCloud account, the app works entirely locally.

3.3 AI features (optional)

The AI-powered features (recipe import from URLs or photos, generating a recipe from a name or from your pantry, nutrition analysis recommendations) are optional. You choose the provider in the app's settings; the default is “Disabled”, in which case no AI features are shown and no data is transmitted for AI purposes at all.

  • Apple Intelligence — processing happens on Apple's on-device model, directly on your device. Your recipe and ingredient data does not leave the device.
  • OpenAI — requires your own OpenAI API key, which you enter voluntarily in the settings. The key is stored exclusively in your device's Keychain and is never transmitted to any third party. When you invoke an AI feature, the content needed for it (e.g. the text of a web page, a recipe photo or ingredient names) is sent to the OpenAI API. Processing by OpenAI is subject to OpenAI's Privacy Policy.

3.4 Camera and Photo Library

The app accesses your camera or photo library when you import a recipe from a photo or scan a product barcode. This is done solely to extract recipe information or to read the barcode. The barcode camera feed is evaluated on the device and is not stored. Recipe photos are not stored permanently and are not shared with third parties — except with the AI provider you selected, if you use the photo import feature (see 3.3).

3.5 Barcode lookup (Open Food Facts)

When you scan a barcode while adding an item, the app looks the product name up via the public API of Open Food Facts. Only the scanned barcode number is transmitted — no account, no identifier and no other content from the app. As with any internet request, your device's IP address is technically visible to the operator. Every item can still be entered by hand; without a scan, no lookup takes place.

3.6 Voice control in cooking mode (optional)

In cooking mode you can enable voice control to move between steps hands-free. Only then does the app access the microphone. Recognition runs entirely on your device — the app explicitly requests on-device recognition and will not start voice control if your device does not support it. Neither audio recordings nor transcripts are stored or transmitted to Apple, to the provider or to any third party.

3.7 Nutrition analysis

Nutrition values come from an extract of the USDA FoodData Central database bundled with the app. The analysis runs entirely on your device; no server is queried for it. Only if you have enabled AI features are ingredient names sent to the AI provider you selected, for translating those names and for the recommendations (see 3.3).

3.8 Notifications

When you use the sharing feature, push notifications may be sent via Apple Push Notification Service (APNs) to notify shared participants about changes to the shopping list. No personal content is transmitted via APNs; Apple only receives a technical device signal.

If you set an expiry date for a pantry item, the app additionally schedules a local reminder on that device. It is triggered by the device itself; no data is transmitted for it.

3.9 Settings

Some settings belong to the household rather than to the device: the default number of people, the order of shopping categories and the learned category assignment of individual items, the household's name and the pantry-integration switch are synchronised via iCloud together with that household's other content (see 3.2). In a shared household they therefore apply to everyone taking part.

Which household is currently active is additionally stored in your private iCloud area, so that your own devices — an Apple Watch, for example — follow the same household. That information is not shared with other people.

Settings that only affect this one device stay purely local to it (UserDefaults): your choice of AI provider, whether completed items are hidden, the selected pantry filter, the cooking-mode voice control settings and whether the introduction has been shown. This data does not leave your device.

4. Legal Basis

The processing of the data described above is based on Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of a contract / provision of app functionality) and Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent), where you activate optional features such as the OpenAI import.

5. Sharing with Third Parties

Your data is not shared with third parties, except for:

  • Apple Inc. — for iCloud synchronisation and push notifications, under Apple's own privacy terms.
  • OpenAI, L.L.C. — only if you have selected OpenAI as your AI provider, entered an API key and explicitly invoked an AI feature.
  • Open Food Facts — the scanned barcode number only, and only when you use the barcode scanner (see 3.5).

6. Retention

Data on your device is retained for as long as the app is installed. You can delete all data at any time within the app or by uninstalling it. iCloud data can additionally be deleted via your device's iCloud settings.

7. Your Rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data held about you (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • Rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • Erasure of your data (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • Restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • Data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • Object to processing (Art. 21 GDPR)
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

Since all data resides locally on your device or in your personal iCloud account, you have direct access for most of these rights through your device and the app itself.

For any requests, contact: pantr@corlatan.de
